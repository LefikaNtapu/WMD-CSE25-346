About Setlhoa Apartments

Setlhoa Village provides modern, affordable and secure living spaces designed for comfort and convenience

Our goal is to provide quality housing that meets the needs of students, professionals and families!

Unit Types: Primarily 2-bedroom (1 to 2 bathroom) and 3-bedroom (2 bathroom) units. Estate Amenities: Include a communal swimming pool, indoor and outdoor gyms, children’s play areas, a convenience store, and a community center .Unit Features:Modern open-plan fitted kitchens, air conditioning, private balconies, and dedicated parking

Our Apartments depending on the one you choose:

  • Wifi included
  • Parking
  • 24/7 Security Monitoring
  • Convienent location (1km away from the nearest high school and shopping center).
